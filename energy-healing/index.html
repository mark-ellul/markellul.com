Frequently asked questions

What is energy healing?+

Energy healing is a holistic practice that works with the body's energy systems. It's based on the idea that disruptions or imbalances in energy flow can affect wellbeing, and that a practitioner can help support the body's natural processes.

What is Reiki?+

Reiki is a Japanese energy healing technique developed in the early 20th century. It involves the practitioner placing their hands lightly on or near the body to channel energy. I've been practising Reiki since 1998.

What happens in a session?+

Sessions are typically conducted in a calm, quiet setting. You remain fully clothed and either sit or lie down. I'll place my hands lightly on or near different areas of your body. Sessions usually last between 45 and 90 minutes.

Do I need to prepare anything?+

No special preparation is needed. Wear comfortable clothing and come with an open mind. It can help to avoid heavy meals or caffeine immediately before a session.

Is energy healing a substitute for medical treatment?+

No. Energy healing is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical or psychological care. Always consult a qualified health professional for medical concerns.
